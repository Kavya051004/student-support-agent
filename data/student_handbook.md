# Greenfield Institute of Management - Student Handbook (SAMPLE / FAKE)

This is a made-up handbook used only for the Agentic AI assignment.
Each rule starts with `###` and an ID such as FEE-01. The agent retrieves
only the few rules that match the student's question.

## Fees

### FEE-01 Fee instalments and due dates
The programme fee is paid in three instalments. The due dates are 15 July, 15 November and 15 March of each academic year. The fee status shown in the student record is one of: Paid, Partially Paid, Pending or Overdue.

### FEE-02 Late payment charges
If an instalment is paid after its due date, a late fee of Rs 100 per day is charged. The late fee is capped at Rs 2,000 per instalment. If an instalment stays unpaid for more than 30 days after the due date, the exam hall ticket is withheld until the dues are cleared.

### FEE-03 Accepted payment modes
Fees can be paid online through the student payment portal using UPI, net banking, debit card or credit card. A demand draft in favour of Greenfield Institute of Management is also accepted at the Accounts Office. Cash is not accepted.

### FEE-04 Payment portal failure
If the payment portal is down or a payment fails on the due date, the student must raise a support request within 1 working day and attach a screenshot or the transaction reference. If the request is raised in time and the Accounts Office confirms the portal failure, the student may pay on the next working day without any late fee. If money was deducted but not credited, it is reconciled or refunded within 7 working days.

### FEE-05 Fee refunds
Tuition fee is refundable only if a student withdraws before the programme starts. A refund of 90 percent is given if the withdrawal request is made at least 15 days before the programme start date. No tuition fee refund is given after the programme has started.

### FEE-06 Fee receipts
A fee receipt is emailed within 2 working days of a successful payment. A duplicate receipt can be requested by raising a support request in the Fees category.

## Scholarships

### SCH-01 Types of scholarships
The institute offers Merit Scholarships (based on the admission score), Need-Based Aid (based on family income documents) and Sponsored Scholarships (paid by a partner company or organisation).

### SCH-02 Scholarship renewal criteria
A Merit Scholarship is renewed every year only if the student keeps a CGPA of at least 7.5 and attendance of at least 80 percent. If either condition is not met, the scholarship is placed under review by the Scholarship Committee.

### SCH-03 How scholarships are paid
Scholarships are never paid in cash. The amount is adjusted against the next fee instalment. The adjustment is made within 10 working days after the term results are published.

### SCH-04 Scholarship appeals
A student can appeal a scholarship decision within 15 days of the decision. The appeal is made by raising a support request in the Scholarship category with supporting documents. The Scholarship Committee replies within 10 working days.

## Courses and academics

### CRS-01 Adding and dropping electives
An elective can be added or dropped within the first 2 weeks of a term by raising a support request to the Academic Office. After the first 2 weeks, an elective can be dropped only with the approval of the Programme Chair, and a W (withdrawn) grade is recorded.

### CRS-02 Core courses
Core courses cannot be dropped or replaced. A student who fails a core course must repeat it in the next offering.

### CRS-03 Change of specialisation
A student may change specialisation only once, at the end of Term 2. The student needs a CGPA of at least 7.0 and the change depends on seat availability in the new specialisation.

### CRS-04 Minimum credits and attendance
Every student must register for at least 18 credits in each term. Attendance of at least 75 percent in each course is required to appear in the end-term exam.

## Support and privacy

### SUP-01 How support requests work
Support requests can be raised through this assistant or the student portal. The categories are Fees, Scholarship, Academic, IT/Portal and Other. Every request is acknowledged within 1 working day and the target resolution time is 3 working days.

### SUP-02 Changing contact details
A change to the registered phone number or email address needs verification and approval by Student Services staff. The change is applied only after approval and can take up to 2 working days. Staff will never ask for an OTP or a password. Students must never share an OTP or a password with anyone, including this assistant.

### SUP-03 Student data privacy
A student can see and change only their own record. Staff access to student records is role based and every access to private data is logged.
